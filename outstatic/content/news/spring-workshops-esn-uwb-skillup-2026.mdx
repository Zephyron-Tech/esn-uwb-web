Let's be honest: University gives you theory. But who teaches you how to *actually* ace a job interview? Or build a website from scratch? Or use AI without it spitting nonsense?

That's exactly why ESN UWB Pilsen launched our **Spring Workshop Series**—six focused sessions designed to bridge the gap between classroom knowledge and career readiness.

Between March and May 2026, **over 80 students registered** and **more than 60 showed up** for hands-on workshops led by industry professionals from QuickJobs, Zephyron Tech, Ant-studio, and Kompetia.

Here's what went down. 👇

---

**Workshop 1: "Jak na 130.000 měsíčně" with Dalibor Herbrich (QuickJobs)**\
📅 March 24 

Dalibor broke down the **brutal truth about CVs and interviews** in the Czech job market. Students learned which keywords HR actually searches for and how to structure interview answers.

**Student take:** *"One of the few workshops that was truly useful and practical."*

---

**Workshop 2: "Mapa IT Světa" with Bc. Jan Vandlíček (Zephyron Tech)**\
📅 April 28 

Ever wondered how apps *actually* work? Jan walked us through **frontend, middleware, and backend architecture** using a live demo of a receipt-splitting app. Students left understanding the difference between Python data processing and Java security layers.

**Student take:** *"You really need to try building things yourself."*

---

**Workshop 3: "Online Marketing Prakticky" with Mgr. Miloš Zlesa (Ant-studio)**\
📅 April 29 

Miloš made online marketing **click** by focusing on strategy over tools. Students learned to identify target audiences, craft compelling CTAs, and measure ROI—skills immediately applicable to part-time gigs or personal projects.

**Student take:** *"This topic was timely for me both professionally and personally."*

---

**Workshop 4: "Speak Without the Script" with Gabriela Šiller (Kompetia)**\
📅 May 13 

Public speaking in English? Terrifying for most. Gabriela's workshop flipped the script with **improv exercises**—including a 3-minute spontaneous monologue challenge that made students realize how long 3 minutes *actually* feels.

**Student take:** *"The unscripted speaking activity was intense—but that's where you learn."*

---

**Workshop 5: "Present Like You Mean It" with Gabriela Šiller (Kompetia)**\
📅 May 21 

Building on workshop 4, this session focused on **structuring presentations for impact**. Key lesson? Start with shock value—grab attention in the first seconds or lose your audience.

**Student take:** *"At the beginning, it's critical to shock or grab attention."*

---

**Workshop 6: "AI Nástroje Pro Každého" with Valerij Lejzo**\
📅 May 21 

AI is everywhere—but are you using it right? Valerij demystified **ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini**, explaining why AI sometimes spits nonsense (spoiler: dialogue window limits) and how to avoid it. Together we built a study app using apple terminal. 

**Student take:** *"Understanding the context window changed how I use AI."*

---

**What's Next?**

This spring series was ambitious—**6 workshops in 8 weeks**. We learned that intensity isn't always sustainable. For next semester, we're refining our approach: **fewer events, deeper engagement.**

But one thing's certain: **We're not stopping.** ESN UWB Pilsen exists to elevate our student community, and career-skills programming is now a core part of that mission.
